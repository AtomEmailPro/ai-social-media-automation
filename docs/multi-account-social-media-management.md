# Multi-Account Social Media Management

Managing multiple social media accounts requires more than simply storing account credentials.

A scalable system needs to organize accounts, campaigns, content, tasks, and publishing schedules.

## Why Multi-Account Management Becomes Difficult

Consider a workflow with 50 accounts.

Each account may have:

* Different content
* Different schedules
* Different campaigns
* Different publishing tasks
* Different statuses

Managing everything manually quickly becomes difficult.

## Account Groups

Account grouping provides a logical way to organize accounts.

```text id="x7d2mb"
Account Groups
│
├── Brand A
│   ├── Account 01
│   ├── Account 02
│   └── Account 03
│
├── Brand B
│   ├── Account 04
│   ├── Account 05
│   └── Account 06
```

Tasks can then be assigned to groups instead of individual accounts.

## Account-Level Configuration

Each account may have its own configuration.

```text id="p6r1nv"
Account
├── Platform
├── Profile
├── Session
├── Proxy
├── Status
└── Assigned Tasks
```

Keeping account-level settings separate makes large account collections easier to manage.

## Campaign Assignment

A campaign can be assigned to an account group.

```text id="3p1gdr"
Campaign A
     ↓
Brand A Account Group
     ↓
Multiple Accounts
```

The system can then generate the required tasks automatically.

## Task Management

Each account can have multiple tasks.

```text id="2s9vbe"
Account 01
├── Publish Post A
├── Publish Post B
└── Publish Post C
```

A task queue keeps these operations organized.

## Status Monitoring

Account and task status should remain visible.

Example:

```text id="g3xq9h"
Account 01 → Active
Account 02 → Active
Account 03 → Attention Required
Account 04 → Task Running
```

For large account collections, visibility is essential.

## Multi-Platform Management

A multi-account workflow can include different platforms:

```text id="8t6j5n"
Account Group
├── Facebook
├── Instagram
├── X
├── TikTok
└── YouTube
```

The same campaign structure can then be adapted to different platforms.

## Reducing Manual Work

The objective is to reduce operations such as:

* Switching between accounts
* Repeating content configuration
* Creating the same publishing tasks
* Checking each account manually
* Tracking schedules separately

Automation moves these repetitive processes into a centralized workflow.

## Conclusion

Multi-account social media management is fundamentally an organization and workflow problem.

Account groups, campaign structures, task queues, scheduling, and status monitoring provide the foundation for a scalable system.
